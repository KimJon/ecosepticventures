The Eco Biodigester System

Employing the principle of Bio-Degradation and Equal Displacement.

How It Works

1

Bio-Degradation

Waste scum and sludge from toilets are completely broken down by both aerobic and anaerobic bacteria permanently present in the BioSeptic to produce clean water.

2

Equal Displacement

The amount of water flushed into the inlet buffer displaces an equal amount out to the French Drain (Soak Pit). The system never fills up, eliminating the need for exhausting.

A Complete System Combines:

  • Thermo Biodigester Tank: Made of high tensile steel mesh and expanded polystyrene panels (insulators).
  • Grease Trap: For grey water from bathrooms and kitchens.
  • Soakpit: Allows clean water absorption back into the ground.

Advantages Over Septic Tanks

  • No Nasty Odour

    Airtight reinforced panels prevent foul odors common in conventional tanks.

  • No Exhausting Needed

    Waste is bio-degraded to produce harmless gases and clean water. It never gets full!

  • Very Economical

    Lower installation and maintenance costs compared to traditional systems.

  • Space Saving

    Occupies a very small space, preserving your property's aesthetics and usability.

  • Versatile Terrain Suitability

    Works well in cotton soil, rocky terrain, redsoil, murram, and sand soil. Strong enough not to be crushed.

  • 100 Year Durability

    Polystyrene material doesn't decompose, ensuring long-lasting performance.
